    How to read this screen

    Each person has one card. The card's colour, symbol and words all say the same thing, so it reads in bright sun or in black and white. Cards never move, so you can find the same person in the same place.

    Three things to remember

    • The pod decides. Alerts come from the pod on the person's arm, using your team's settings. This screen only shows them.
    • "Got it" is a note, not a dismissal. It records your name and the time. The alert stays until the pod sees the person is back within the settings.
    • Consent first. The hub records nothing from a pod until that person opts in, and stops within seconds if they opt out.

    Body Heat is a general wellness product. It compares skin temperature with the settings your team chose. It does not diagnose anything.
